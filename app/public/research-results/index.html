04 / What the experiments establish

Keep the evidence standards visible

BEST SO FAR

The baselines still lead

Five regex revisions and the first two completed Qwen revisions did not pass their improvement gates. The audited V3 continuation also regressed on shared cases.

REAL DATA FIRST

Synthetic tests stay separate

They test numerical boundaries and misleading language. They are not estimates of real market coverage.

EMAIL ≠ ARTICLE

Track each evidence source

A linked article is not part of the email’s DKIM-authenticated body. Measure article and email performance separately.

The original baselines remain the provisional best completed methods. V3’s separate continuation preserved four unknown responses; its original run remains incomplete. On 143 shared factual pairs, exact-quote hits fell from 55 to 44. This work changes prompts and procedures, not Qwen’s weights.
