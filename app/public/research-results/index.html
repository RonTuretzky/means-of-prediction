03 / The unanswered question

Which emails can actually
close a market?

The current labels say that an email reports a relevant fact. They do not establish that it meets every settlement requirement.

01 — SOURCE

Is NYT sufficient?

Some rules require a specific official source or a broader reporting consensus.

02 — TIME & EVENT

Is this the right result?

Match the event, stage, deadline and finality. Distinguish event time from evidence availability.

03 — EXCEPTIONS

Does the rule permit it?

Apply cancellation, fallback and nonbinary outcomes without inventing a Yes or No.

The accuracy on definitively settleable real pairs is still unknown. The 39% and 47% results cannot be presented as that answer.

Reference: Polymarket resolution rules. The title asks the question; the full rules determine resolution.

04 / What the experiments establish

Keep the evidence standards visible

BEST SO FAR

The baselines still lead

Five regex revisions and the first two completed Qwen revisions did not pass their improvement gates.

REAL DATA FIRST

Synthetic tests stay separate

They test numerical boundaries and misleading language. They are not estimates of real market coverage.

EMAIL ≠ ARTICLE

Track each evidence source

A linked article is not part of the email’s DKIM-authenticated body. Measure article and email performance separately.

This snapshot reflects the original baselines as the provisional best completed methods. V3 was incomplete at the snapshot. The work has optimized prompts and procedures; it has not changed Qwen’s model weights.
