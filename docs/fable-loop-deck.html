Why this loop exists

Can a public prediction market settle from NYT evidence?

Path A · frozen

Deterministic regex rules

Generated blind from public market terms, matched against the email body on chain. Round 4 reached a documented plateau and is frozen; its baseline recovered 63 of 161 known facts.

Path B · this loop

Plain-language rules + local judge

The generator writes factual and settlement predicates from public terms only. A local Qwen judge reads one whole email plus the frozen rule and returns a strict JSON judgment. The Astra baseline recovered 55 of 161 with an exact quote.

What is being optimized

Prompts, never weights. A teacher reads complete development shards and proposes a new generator prompt and judge prompt. Each revision is a new method with frozen prompts, drawn and judged from scratch, then gated against the baseline. Only one real development pair is answerable under its original market rules, so this measures factual recovery, not lawful settlement.

Information boundaries

Who sees what

Generator

Public terms only

Market ID, question, rules, ordered outcome labels, optional public context, trial number. Never an email, payout, label or per-case answer. Residual harness context: the signed-in email reminder and an environment block with the date.

Judge

One rule, one whole email

The frozen rule and the complete semantic rendering of a single email with its metadata. No labels, no tools, no history, no other emails.

Teacher / optimizer

Train rows, complete evidence

Every train case with its email, rule, raw judgment and score. Validation rows are withheld. Prompts it returns must pass the lexical review before use.

Evaluation reservations

The sealed 12-question / 120-fixture evaluation and the supplemental natural annotations stay closed. They open only after a selection, a 48-draw freeze and the declared gates, none of which the loop runs.

New in this round

A sealed validation split decides which revision to build on

How the units are formed

A factual market travels with its whole development group: every market in the group and every control, weak and unlabeled row on those markets. Other markets are their own unit. Units are visited in SHA-256 order and added to validation until the targets are met, never exceeding the caps. Sealed before the first hosted call.

Selection rule

A revision is allowed only if, on validation rows, it passes the existing eligibility gates, shows strict paired improvement on rows both it and the baseline completed, and clears predeclared margins: at least +3 grounded factual passes and +8 strict positive passes, with false positives not above baseline. The leader is the allowed method with the highest validation utility, else baseline. The next revision chains from the leader, because every Astra revision that chained from a regressed predecessor regressed further.

Disclosed limitation. The rows form one connected component through shared emails. Validation market labels are withheld, but validation email bodies can still appear in train rows of other markets, and the optimizer packet carries full-cohort aggregates.

Teacher shards are the heavy part: roughly 80 shards of complete emails per method, each well over a hundred thousand input tokens. That is where sign-in limits will bite.

Running unattended

Limits are gates, not failures. Everything else is a receipt.

Sign-in usage limits

A limited call records the receipt, publishes a shared gate so the other workers pause instead of burning requests, sleeps until the reset named in the receipt plus two minutes, then resubmits the same frozen request. Every attempt is recorded. At most four waits and six hours per call; only an unwaitable limit trips the persistent stop marker.

Immutability and resume

Artifacts are write-once. Driver phases leave started and completed markers; a relaunch skips completed phases and refuses to touch an uncertain one. Nothing is deleted; a stop marker is retained under a numbered name with a reconciliation note before any relaunch.

Stop conditions
  • Wall-clock projection past the 12-hour deadline before a judge pass starts (1.15 × last judge wall + 45 min).
  • Revision cap of three; two consecutive revisions not allowed on validation; a failed prompt review.
  • An incomplete rule cohort, local judge failures above 1 %, any failed phase, or an unwaitable usage stop.
  • Launch guards: pinned 2.1.263 Claude Code binary by hash, exactly one loaded LM Studio model with the pinned context, no other round processes, an owner lock on the root.

Reading the first result

The baseline's grounded passes out of 161 land next to Astra's 55. That comparison is a like-for-like generator swap on identical prompts, cohort, judge and seed. It is one development cohort, not an accuracy estimate for any market population.

What happens next

The loop produces candidates. Promotion is a separate decision.

Automatic
  • Per-method development summaries, training audit, report, validation selection with leader and allowed list.
  • Every hosted receipt: request hash, session ID, usage, cost, stop reason, waits.
  • A stop marker naming the reason the loop ended.
Only on explicit decision
  • select intersects validation and full-development gates and seals the root.
  • 48 fresh draws for baseline and challenger, freeze, then open the sealed 120-fixture evaluation.
  • Any live settlement promotion, which nothing in this loop performs.
Standing caveats

Only one of 161 real pairs is answerable under its original market rules. Factual recovery is not settlement. Temperature-0 replay of the judge under 4-way concurrency agreed on 50 of 55 strict decisions, so deltas below that noise floor are not interpretable. Full NYT article bodies are still unavailable; the evidence corpus is newsletter email.
