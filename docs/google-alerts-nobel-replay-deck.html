A real market, three real alerts.

“Nobel Peace Prize 2026 awarded to an individual?”

Costs, simply

More emails make verification more expensive.

Split the upload

Send a large email in smaller pieces.

This helps with size limits. It can cost more overall.

Use a zero-knowledge (ZK) proof

Prove that the email checks and rule checks were done correctly.

This may reduce on-chain verification cost. We need to benchmark it.

ZK can make a proof smaller. It cannot supply a missing fact or make an unreliable story true.
